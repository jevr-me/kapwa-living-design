# Case 001 — Holy Paradise Resort

Siquijor, Philippines. See the foundational publication, pp. 20–34.

This preliminary design study is grounded in the founder's account of the place, not a claim of scientific validation. It distinguishes existing conditions from proposals and hypotheses.

The case examines landscape, built environment, water, energy, food and economic flows, design alternatives, implementation and learning, food production and animal care, and cultural knowledge and education.

The Center serves as a restaurant and function hall. Food production and animal care include bananas and papaya, poultry, native bee hives and rabbits. Protect forage and habitat, observe colony health and pollination, provide suitable shelter and feed, and manage manure safely. The irrigation distribution tank receives pumped stream water; it is distinct from the gravity-fed spring pool system.

Engineering, public health, ecological and legal decisions require appropriate assessments and expertise.
