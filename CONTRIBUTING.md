# Contributing to KLD

Anyone may study, use, teach, translate, critique, adapt and build upon KLD under its license. Independent branches need not merge into the official framework.

For proposed contributions, open an issue or pull request identifying the version, concrete design question, problem, suggested revision and supporting experience. Show how you used the framework, distinguish observations from interpretations, and explain what was learned.

Respect privacy, consent and community control over knowledge. Do not submit restricted or sacred knowledge without authorization. Identify adaptations, credit sources, and use distinct branding for independent branches.

The founder initially stewards the official version with a public revision history. See [license](LICENSE.md).
